Business August 19, 2026

UNACEM North America contributes to Grupo UNACEM’s strong Q2 2026 performance.

U.S. revenue reached USD 123.4 million in the second quarter of 2026, supported by higher volumes in cementitious materials, ready mix and aggregates.

U.S. revenue reached USD 123.4 million in the second quarter of 2026, 21.1% higher than the same quarter in 2025. U.S. EBITDA was USD 10.7 million.

Cementitious material shipments increased 11.8%, ready mix volumes increased 27.0% and aggregates volumes increased 55.6%.

Scheduled maintenance at the U.S. cement plants was completed during the first quarter of 2026.

Source: Grupo UNACEM Q2 2026 results release. Editorial review required before publication.
